Meta media buying Stakeholder report 28 October 2026

Executive Summary & Rationale

We conducted a comparative analysis between Niiidental Studio and East Credit Dental.

  • Rationale: East Credit Dental is currently running an Invisalign campaign that is producing exceptional lead results. To uncover actionable insights, we compared Niiidental Studio's lower-performing Invisalign campaign against this winning benchmark.
  • Analysis Period: September 28 – October 27, 2026 (30-day performance window).
  • Objective: Determine which specific performance metrics in East Credit Dental's ad account are driving its superior results relative to Niiidental Studio.

Bottom line

The largest observed difference is in post-click lead-event rates.

Link click-through rates and video engagement are similar. The largest observed difference is after the click: 5.88% of Niiidental's landing-page views resulted in a recorded lead event, compared with 16.33% for East Credit.

Based on the campaigns' recorded Lead events, cost per lead is $64.57 USD for Niiidental and $22.49 CAD for East Credit. The reported costs use different currencies; convert them before interpreting the difference as a financial comparison.

Niiidental's account spent $931.91 across the window. Of that amount, $415.34 went to the Summer Special, which ended on 9 October. How to use this comparison

Compare CPM, click costs, landing-page views, recorded lead events and cost per lead together. A lower media cost does not necessarily produce a lower cost per lead. Confirm the event definitions before attributing the difference to the website or the campaign.

Method

How these numbers were produced

Funnel order

Results are reviewed in this order: link CTR, cost per link click, landing-page view rate, recorded lead events per landing-page view, and cost per lead. CPM and frequency provide context on delivery costs; they do not by themselves explain conversion results.

Why link CTR is used

CTR (all) was 1.41% against 1.12%, and it is not headlined here because it counts reactions, comments and profile taps as well as clicks. Link CTR focuses on people who clicked through to the site.

Currencies

East Credit reports in CAD and Niiidental in USD. Rates within each account do not require conversion. The 2.87× cost-per-lead multiple is a face-value comparison; convert currencies before using it as a financial comparison.

Sample

East Credit recorded 40 Lead events from 276 link clicks; Niiidental recorded 8 from 165. Niiidental's lower event count is a limited sample for judging performance.

Buckets

Weekly rates are recomputed from summed counts within each week rather than averaging daily percentages. The 26–27 October bucket covers two days and is marked in the report.

What would change the read

Consistent pixel Lead-event tracking and additional Niiidental data. These could change the size or interpretation of the reported difference.
